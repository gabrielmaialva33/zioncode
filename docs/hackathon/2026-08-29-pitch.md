# Zion ARC hackathon pitch

Date: 2026-08-29

## Tagline and one-liner

**Knowledge that travels as art.**

Zion ARC is an open, versioned exact-PNG transport protocol that seals one
offline knowledge item into one piece of phone artwork and opens it in a
network-free Android reader using established cryptography.

ARC is a new protocol composition and framing design, not a new cipher. The
passphrase is the secret; the algorithm is public.

## Three-minute pitch

### 0:00–0:35 — The problem

“Offline knowledge distribution still makes people choose between recognizable
data containers, fragile links, and special hardware. We asked a narrower
question: can one complete, independently recoverable document travel inside a
normal-looking phone wallpaper and still open without a cloud account?”

“Zion ARC's first legal demonstration uses the 66-book Portuguese BLIVRE
release. Each book is its own capsule, so the reader imports one original PNG,
asks for a passphrase, authenticates it, and renders the book offline.”

### 0:35–1:15 — What was built

“ARC normalizes a document, compresses the whole item with zstd, seals an
encrypted envelope with XChaCha20-Poly1305, adds Reed–Solomon protection,
interleaves it, and places the result in keyed RGB channel positions. Argon2id
makes password guesses expensive. The ordering matters: error correction is
outside the authenticated ciphertext, so bounded damage can be repaired before
authentication.”

“This does not replace cryptographic primitives. It defines how established,
widely reviewed primitives, bounded parsing, metadata, capacity, and an exact
lossless carrier fit together.”

### 1:15–1:55 — Measured proof

“The pinned BLIVRE importer produced exactly 66 books, 1,189 chapters, and
31,102 verse records. Every normalized book fits the 1080×2340 Safe-profile
reference carrier. Psalms is the largest: 657,274 JSON bytes, 123,349 bytes
after ARC zstd, and 556 codewords out of 1,226.”

“On the final artwork run, the sealed PNG was 3,972,714 bytes. A deliberately
less-compressed, still-lossless PNG container was 7,607,311 bytes. Their PNG
hashes differed, but all 7,581,600 RGB samples were identical and both opened
to the same 657,274-byte JSON. Clean-versus-sealed quality measured 59.387568 dB
PSNR and 0.998874 SSIM.”

### 1:55–2:25 — Phone and studio story

“The Android MVP is arm64-first, imports original PNG bytes through Android's
document picker, calls the Rust core over one byte-oriented JNI function, and
requests no internet permission. The APK, lint, host tests, and arm64 native
build pass. Physical-device and performance validation remain pending and are
not part of today's measured claim.”

“AI and an RTX 4090 are studio tools for creating and evaluating diverse,
texture-rich covers. They never see the passphrase during reading, perform no
encryption, and are not required on the phone. Remove every model and GPU and
the same capsule still opens.”

### 2:25–3:00 — Product and close

“The business is not a secret cipher. The protocol and implementation can stay
inspectable while Zion sells integration SDKs, white-label readers, deployment
tooling, provenance and collection workflows, engineering support, and funded
security evaluation. Customers can use the same transport for educational,
archival, emergency, or cultural material they are authorized to distribute.”

“Zion ARC turns an exact lossless image into a self-contained offline delivery
surface: one artwork, one authenticated item, no reader-side cloud. Knowledge
that travels as art.”

## Ninety-second demo

This is the intended live order after a physical arm64 device has passed the
documented acceptance run. Until then, use the same original PNG with the
desktop CLI and explicitly call it a desktop fallback.

| Time | Action | Spoken proof |
|---:|---|---|
| 0–12 s | Enable airplane mode; show the ARC PNG in the system Files app. | “The reader has no internet permission and receives the original PNG byte stream.” |
| 12–28 s | In Zion ARC, choose the PNG through `OpenDocument("image/png")`. | “There is no QR scanner, camera pipeline, or `Bitmap` re-encode.” |
| 28–43 s | Enter the passphrase and open Psalms. | “Rust decodes the RS layer—repairing only if actual damage is within the selected per-codeword budget—then authenticates, decompresses, validates the book JSON, and only then returns content.” |
| 43–55 s | Scroll chapters and open About/Licenses. | “This is pinned BLIVRE 2018.2.0 under CC BY 3.0 BR; the original USFM remains exportable.” |
| 55–68 s | Try a wrong passphrase against a fresh output path. | “Recovery fails generically and returns no plaintext.” |
| 68–80 s | Show the metrics record and the 3.97 MB / 7.61 MB pair. | “Different PNG bytes, exact same sealed RGB raster, exact same recovered JSON.” |
| 80–90 s | Show the 66-row fit table. | “All 66 books fit independently; Psalms still has 670 Safe codewords of margin.” |

Do not substitute a screenshot, gallery-edited image, messaging-app copy,
resized rendition, or JPEG for the original ARC PNG. A bounded-corruption demo
may be added only with a generated fixture whose actual per-codeword damage is
measured and remains inside the documented Safe limit.

## Architecture

```text
Studio / collection builder
  pinned legal source -> deterministic book JSON -> zstd level 6
  -> Argon2id collection key
  -> XChaCha20-Poly1305 authenticated envelope
  -> RS(255,223) Safe ECC -> column-major interleave
  -> keyed LSB matching in exact 1080x2340 RGB8 artwork
  -> lossless ARC PNG

Offline Android reader
  OpenDocument PNG bytes -> bounded JNI byte array -> Rust ARC open
  -> RS repair -> AEAD verification -> bounded zstd decode
  -> validated canonical book JSON -> Compose reader / raw USFM export
```

The public `ZARC` bootstrap carries version, profile, random identifiers, and
bounded ciphertext geometry. It deliberately makes capsule existence
detectable and correlates capsules sharing a collection ID. Semantic metadata,
attribution, and content are inside the encrypted envelope.

Authentication does not cover the entire visual file. ARC authenticates the
bootstrap and dimensions as associated data plus the encrypted envelope and
recovered content. The visible cover, upper seven RGB bits, unused channels,
alpha if accepted by some noncanonical tool, and PNG ancillary metadata are
untrusted presentation data.

## A57-class measured capacity

The reference is a 1080×2340 RGB8 portrait asset suitable for the intended
Galaxy A57-class demo. This table is protocol geometry, not a claim about
physical display sampling or device performance.

| Safe-profile measurement | Value |
|---|---:|
| RGB channels | 7,581,600 |
| Usable placement bits | 2,501,759 |
| Maximum codewords | 1,226 |
| Maximum ciphertext | 273,398 bytes |
| Psalms codewords | 556 |
| Psalms ciphertext | 123,988 bytes |
| Psalms remaining codewords | 670 |
| All canonical BLIVRE books fit | 66 of 66 |

PNG file bytes are a storage and presentation choice, not ARC capacity. The
7.61 MB compression-level-0 file carries exactly the same sealed raster and
capacity as the 3.97 MB encoding.

## Business model

The commercial boundary is service and integration, not algorithm secrecy:

- stable protocol and Rust/mobile SDK integration;
- white-label offline readers and institution-specific UX;
- collection building, legal-provenance, validation, and release tooling;
- deployment support, training, maintenance, and compatibility work;
- funded cryptographic review, steganalysis evaluation, and hardening; and
- custom support for authorized educational, archival, emergency, and cultural
  collections.

The BLIVRE corpus is third-party CC BY 3.0 BR material and is not proprietary
Zion content. Code and corpus licenses remain separate.

## Judges' questions

### Is this a new encryption algorithm?

No. ARC is a transport protocol that composes Argon2id, BLAKE3,
XChaCha20-Poly1305, zstd, Reed–Solomon, interleaving, and keyed placement. It has
not received an independent cryptographic audit.

### Is the capsule invisible?

No. The public `ZARC` bootstrap makes existence detectable, and statistical
steganalysis is outside the current proof. The artwork is a carrier and user
experience, not a promise of forensic deniability.

### Why not use a QR code?

The demo transports a complete compressed book in a full-resolution lossless
image and presents it as artwork. It is a different exact-file workflow, not a
camera-readable replacement for every QR use case.

### Does the 7.61 MB PNG hold more data than the 3.97 MB PNG?

No. Both files decode to the same sealed RGB samples. ARC capacity comes from
dimensions, the 33% placement ceiling, profile, and metadata—not PNG
compression ratio.

### Will WhatsApp, Instagram, a screenshot, JPEG, or a printed photo work?

No guarantee. JPEG, screenshot, resize, crop, print/camera, color correction,
and social-media recompression are unsupported and may destroy the capsule.

### What does the RTX 4090 do?

It can accelerate studio-side generation and evaluation of future carrier
variants. The committed demonstration asset came through OpenAI's built-in
image generation, with its exact prompt recorded. Neither that service nor the
4090 participates in sealing cryptography or recovery.

### Is the visible image authenticated?

Not as a whole. ARC authenticates its bootstrap/dimensions and encrypted
content, not cover appearance, upper RGB bits, unused pixels, or PNG metadata.

### Is the Bible content yours?

No. The demo uses the pinned BLIVRE 2018.2.0 release, attributes its three
authors, preserves byte-exact source USFM, and records CC BY 3.0 BR provenance.

### Does this authorize carrying restricted material across a border?

No. ARC supplies no legal authorization and no advice to bypass inspection or
import rules. Operators remain responsible for content rights and local law.

## Prohibited claims

Do not call ARC a novel or secret cipher, unbreakable, military-grade,
post-quantum, formally verified, independently audited, undetectable,
steganalysis-resistant, or plausibly deniable. Do not promise recovery from a
JPEG, screenshot, resize, crop, print, camera capture, color conversion, or
social-media copy. Do not claim physical A57 validation, a three-cover Android
demo, or a measured corruption count until those exact tests have happened.

Use the precise current claim: ARC provides offline confidentiality for a
strong passphrase, authenticated recovery, a measured per-codeword ECC budget,
and exact lossless RGB8 PNG round trips under the published threat model.
